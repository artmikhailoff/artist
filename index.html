Biography

Sergei Ivanovich Kasabov

Sergei Ivanovich Kasabov (Касабов Сергей Иванович) was born on 15 May 1951 in Baku and lives in Moscow. On international auctions he is sold as Serge (Sergey) Kasabian; Russian catalogues sometimes spell the name «Касабян».

In 1972 he graduated from the Baku Art College. From 1976 to 1989 he worked in artistic casting and illustrated books.

In 1992 he took part in a painting auction in Paris. Since 1995 he has been a member of the Professional Creative Union of Artists of Russia. His paintings are held in private collections in the United States, Germany, France, and Japan.

Documented auction appearances

  • Rossini, Paris — 25 January 2013 «Angels», 1990, 90×120 cm, estimate €500–800. lotsearch.net
  • Giraudeau, Tours (France) — January 2011 «Composition», oil on canvas, 60×90 cm, estimate €200–250. hdv-giraudeau.fr
  • Trinity Auctions, Avon, Connecticut — 6 November 2021 Untitled work, 90×90 cm, estimate $800–1,200. invaluable.com
  • askART / other houses — 2012–2019 Additional lots at Digard (Paris) and Auktionshaus Blank (Germany). artinvestment.ru lists 18 works in total. askART

Still unconfirmed in open catalogues: the exact name of the 1992 Paris auction, sales in London or New York auction houses, and specific Moscow galleries.
